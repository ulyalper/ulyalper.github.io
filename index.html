About me

Coordinating multidisciplinary models with precision.

I specialise in BIM coordination, model quality control and the development of efficient workflows for architectural, structural and MEP teams. Alongside coordination, I build Revit templates, families and internal standards, and develop Dynamo automation to streamline scheduling and documentation — helping teams save time and stay consistent.
